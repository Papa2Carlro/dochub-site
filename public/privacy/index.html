Privacy

This page covers the public Doc Hub website and how we think about data for the desktop app.

Product site

The marketing site is a static SPA on Cloudflare Pages. Privacy-friendly analytics may load when configured at build time:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics (VITE_CF_BEACON_TOKEN) — preferred, no cookies
  • Plausible (VITE_PLAUSIBLE_DOMAIN) — optional, no cookies
  • Google Analytics 4 (VITE_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID) — optional; uses Google’s measurement cookies / signals when enabled. Leave unset to keep the site on privacy-friendly beacons only.

We do not sell personal data or run ads on this site. An optional waitlist may send your email to Formspree only to notify you about releases.

Desktop app

Doc Hub is local-first. Planning and docs live on your machine. In-app analytics buffers default to local-only with sync off unless you turn something on later. We do not require an account for the free baseline.
