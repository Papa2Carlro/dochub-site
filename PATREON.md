### Ember — $3

### Patron — $5
Everything in Ember, plus early changelog / build notes for Doc Hub & studio.

### Anvil — $10
Everything in Patron, plus your name on a future Supporters wall (site).

### Founding Forge — $25
Everything in Anvil, plus a soft vote on what to prioritize next; featured on Supporters.

### Papa Carlo — $50
Everything in Founding Forge, plus a personal thanks credit in a release note / press blurb when relevant.
